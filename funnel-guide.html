3The offer

The promise

"Live Luxe will earn you more than your long-term rent within 12 months — or we pay you the difference plus $10,000."

It works because it attacks the one fear that stops landlords switching: what if short-stay earns less than my reliable tenant? Rules for how we sell it to investors:

  • Short and direct. 3–6 minutes. No "secrets", no hype.
  • Daniel on camera. Filmed in one of our premium apartments. The person in the video is the person on the call.
  • No value stacks. Never "worth $10k, yours for $1k". Investors read it as lying.
  • Say the fee. The management fee is in the video. Fewer calls, but every call already accepted the price.
  • Real numbers, under-claimed. The guarantee is the bold part; everything around it is sober and true.
  • One call to action. Answer six questions, book a call.

Decisions needed before anything else

  • What "beat your LTR rate" measures: net paid to the owner after our fee vs their current lease, or vs an independent appraisal.
  • Is the $10k on top of the shortfall, or the cap?
  • Eligibility: which suburbs and buildings, minimum current rent, furnished or not, lease ending within how many months.
  • The fee we state.

5Test the hook before filming

Five image ads, each saying a completely different thing, one week, small budget. The cheapest cost-per-lead wins and becomes the video's opening. This stops us filming a polished video around an angle nobody cares about.

AngleAd to test
The benefit"Your apartment could earn more than your rent — guaranteed, or we pay you $10k."
The fear"Rates, strata and interest went up again. Your rent didn't."
The proof"We run 80+ CBD apartments on the same floors as yours. Here's what they earn."
The industry"Your property manager gets paid the same whether your apartment earns $650 or $1,100 a week."

The VSL page, as it should read

Melbourne apartment owners only

Your apartment will earn more than your long-term rent within 12 months.Or we pay you the difference, plus $10,000.

Live Luxe runs short-stay apartments in the CBD, Docklands and Southbank, on the same floors as yours. In the four-minute video, Daniel shows you what they earn, what we charge, and how the guarantee works.

See if your apartment qualifies

Six questions, about a minute. No email needed to watch the video. If it fits, you talk to Daniel — the person in it.

Under it: a four-cell proof row (apartments managed · years operating · guest rating · average weekly lift over rent), one owner quote, and the guarantee in full — "we compare what we pay you, after our fee, with the rent on your current lease. If after 12 months you are behind, we pay the shortfall and $10,000 on top. Written into the management agreement." Then the survey, headed Apply now.

The sorry page says it straight: "The guarantee only works for apartments in the buildings and areas we already run, above a minimum rent. That can change, and we would rather tell you straight than waste your time." One optional field for the lease end date, and a line promising contact about two months before.

Check every page on a phone before anything else. Duplicate the VSL page for ad traffic (/apply-ads) so headlines can be tested without touching the main one.

What to sign up for, and when

Timing beats discounts

There are no real GoHighLevel coupon codes — almost every "verified coupon" page is an affiliate link dressed as a discount. The genuine savings are free tiers, annual billing, first-month offers, and the 30-day partner trial we are using instead of the standard 14 days. Even with 30 days, don’t start the clock early: research, hook testing, scripting and filming take about three weeks before anyone builds in GoHighLevel. Started on build-week day one, the 30 days covers the five build days, the vet and the test run, and roughly the first three weeks of ads — so the first invoice arrives at about the same time as the week-4 go/no-go. 10.2 Domain & email sending

Funnel subdomainSettings → Domains → Connect domainJivonz + GoDaddy access
  1. Enter owners.liveluxe.com.au. GHL's GoDaddy one-click authorisation only works from the login that holds the zone; otherwise choose add records manually — it's one CNAME: host owners, value the target GHL shows.
  2. Verify. Then Sites → Funnels → (the funnel) → Settings: select this domain and set the VSL page as the home step (after 10.9).

Do not touch

The liveluxe.com.au zone carries live Google Workspace mail. Add records only for the two subdomains; never edit or delete the existing MX, SPF or DKIM records on the root, and never point the root domain at GHL — that would take down whatever the main domain serves and could break mail.

Dedicated sending domainSettings → Email Services → Dedicated domainJivonz
  1. Enter lc.liveluxe.com.au (not mail. or email.). GHL lists the records: two TXT, one CNAME, two MX, one DMARC TXT — all on the lc subdomain, so they can't collide with Workspace.
  2. Add them in GoDaddy, refresh until every row is green, click Verify domain.
  3. Three dots → Set headers: from name Daniel · Live Luxe, from email daniel@liveluxe.com.au. Every workflow email inherits this.

Warm-up rule

A new sending domain that blasts goes straight to spam. 300 sends on day one, doubling daily. The funnel's own volume is fine; do not import the existing owner list and email it from here in week one.

14House rules

  • Name everything with a number prefix, keep it in folders — 01 Owner survey, 01 Daniel discovery call, 01 VSL funnel. In six months there will be twenty of each.
  • Saved is not published. Pages and workflows both have a separate publish step; "my automation isn't working" is nearly always this.
  • Never edit a query key or a system field. Make a new field.
  • Move cards with the dropdown, never drag.
  • One-way calendar sync. Two-way puts every owner's Zoom into Daniel's personal calendar as a contact and doubles the reminders.
  • Only subdomains touch GHL. The root domain's mail records are off limits.
  • Test in incognito with a real phone number before spending on ads, and after every workflow change.
  • Every form that takes a phone number has the two consent boxes and links to privacy and terms, and the phone is never required.
  • Build it, break it. The execution logs and the workflow Status tab tell you where a contact is stuck.
