The offer

"We'll earn you more than your long-term rent within 12 months — or we pay you the difference plus $10,000."

How we sell it to investors

  • 3–6 minute video. Direct, no hype.
  • Daniel on camera, filmed in one of our premium apartments. The person in the video is the person on the call.
  • Say the management fee in the video.
  • Real numbers, under-claimed. The guarantee is the bold part.
  • One ask: answer six questions, book a call.

David decides first

  1. What "beat your rent" measures: net to owner vs current lease, or vs an appraisal.
  2. $10k on top of the shortfall, or the cap.
  3. Eligibility: suburbs, minimum rent, furnished, lease-end window.
  4. The fee we state.
  5. Which apartment Daniel films in, and the ElevenLabs voice for the narrated parts.
  6. Budget and the week-4 kill number.
  7. When his VSL contact reviews: script stage, or finished pages.

—Platforms and how they connect

Three lanes. Make the video (top), run the funnel (middle, all inside GoHighLevel), measure (bottom). Arrows are the real connections: a video file, an embed code, a DNS record, a pixel, a calendar sync. The detail for every tool and connection is at the bottom of the page.

—Order of work, about four weeks

  1. Research — six answers about us; owners' own words from PropertyChat, Reddit and competitor reviews. Jivonz, 2 days
  2. Test the hook — five image ads, one week, cheapest lead wins. Jivonz, 1 week
  3. Script — copy the structure of one proven VSL, three AI drafts, edit one, three openings. Written to be spoken by Daniel. Jivonz + Daniel, David and his VSL contact approve
  4. Film — Daniel on camera in a premium apartment: the VSL body, three openings, the thank-you and reminder pieces, a behind-the-scenes short. ElevenLabs voice-over for the numbers and slide sections. Daniel, 1 day filming; Jivonz edits, 3 days
  5. Build — GoHighLevel: pipeline, survey, Daniel's calendar, five pages, four workflows. Jivonz, 5 days
  6. Vet — David's VSL contact reviews script, video and pages; fix what they flag. 1–2 days
  7. Test — every path with a real phone; David walks it on his. 1 day
  8. Ads — three ads, $50/day, one test a week; go/no-go at week 4. Jivonz + David
